Scope: the whole app (index.html), Operate mode. Audience: dashcam owner at a laptop with the SD card in. Task: find a moment, play front+rear in sync, step frames, save a frame, see the route.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The moment is the tile. Refuses the raw file list: front and rear of one timestamp are one tile, grouped under day headings, like a photo library groups a day.
OWN-WORLD: Neutral graphite media library. Restrained: graphite ground, one lighter panel step for the sidebar, one blue accent for selection, focus and the primary action. Event red and parking amber only as small state tags. System UI sans, tabular figures. Small 6px radii, no shadows at rest, no gradients, no glass.
STORY: Open the folder once, see days, spot the red event tag, open it, scrub, step frame by frame, save the frame.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Empty: one centred open-folder panel with the local-only promise and a reopen-last-folder action. Loaded: sidebar (folder, camera and type filters, day list) left; right a sticky day heading over a 16:9 thumbnail grid, rear camera inset in the corner of each tile.
FORM: Canon (category standard, user-chosen), seed a2e5f9d1.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
