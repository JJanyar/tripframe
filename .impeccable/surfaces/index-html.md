Scope: the app (app/index.html), Operate mode. Audience: dashcam owner at a laptop with the SD card in. Task: find a moment, play front+rear in sync, step frames, save a frame or a video of the view, see the route.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The moment is the tile. Refuses the raw file list: front and rear of one timestamp are one tile, grouped under day headings, like a photo library groups a day.
OWN-WORLD: Tripframe brand kit on a calm media-library layout. Chalk (light) or asphalt (dark) grounds with warm-grey tone steps, ink for actions, signal yellow as the single mark (progress, event tag, resize grips, map marker), red only while recording. Manrope, tabular figures, 6px radii, no shadows at rest, no gradients, no glass. The viewer is always dark.
STORY: Open the folder once, see days, spot the yellow event tag, open it, swap cameras, step frame by frame, save the frame or record the view.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Empty: one centred open-folder panel with the local-only promise and a reopen-last-folder action. Loaded: sidebar (logo, folder, camera and type filters, day list that follows the scroll) left; right a sticky day heading over a 16:9 thumbnail grid with the rear camera inset in each tile.
FORM: Canon (category standard, user-chosen), seed a2e5f9d1, restyled with the user's brand kit.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
