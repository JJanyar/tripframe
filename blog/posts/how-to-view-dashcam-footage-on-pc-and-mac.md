Something happened on the road and you want to see it on a big screen. Here is the quickest way, for any dashcam brand.

## Step 1: get the footage onto your computer

**Use the memory card.** Turn the car off so the camera stops writing, then take out the microSD card. Most laptops have an SD slot: use the full-size adapter that came with the card, or a cheap USB card reader.

Some cameras store footage internally (the 70mai M500, for example). Connect them with a USB data cable and they show up as a drive. Teslas record to a USB drive: see [TeslaCam footage](/blog/tesla-dashcam-viewer/).

Avoid copying a whole drive over the camera's Wi-Fi app: it is slow.

## Step 2: find your clip

Inside the card you will usually find a `DCIM` folder, sometimes with subfolders for normal, event and parking recordings. The files have names like `2024_0315_143022_000123F.MP4`. These names contain the date and time, but in a form that is hard to scan. See [dashcam file names explained](/blog/dashcam-file-names-explained/).

The quickest way to find a moment is a viewer that reads those names for you.

## Step 3: open it in a dashcam viewer

[Tripframe](/app/) is free and runs in the browser on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS.

1. Open the app in Chrome or Edge (Firefox and Safari also work, through a folder picker).
2. Click **Open your dashcam folder** and choose the card.
3. Pick the day, then the moment.

Front, rear and interior cameras of the same moment play together. Use `,` and `.` to step frame by frame and `S` to save a still.

## Step 4: save what matters

The camera will overwrite the card. Copy the clips you need to your computer, including the matching rear file. Read [how to save dashcam footage for insurance and police](/blog/save-dashcam-footage-for-insurance/).

## Troubleshooting

- **Black video, sound only, or an error:** the camera probably records in H.265. See [how to play H.265 dashcam video](/blog/play-h265-hevc-dashcam-video/).
- **Card not showing up:** try another reader. If the camera says the card has errors, replace it: [the best SD cards for dashcams](/blog/best-sd-cards-for-dashcams/).
- **Clips missing:** check that the camera was not still writing when you removed the card, and that parking mode is set up the way you think: [parking mode explained](/blog/dashcam-parking-mode-explained/).
