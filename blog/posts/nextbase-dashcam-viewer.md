Nextbase is the best-known dashcam brand in the UK and sells widely across Europe and the US. Its desktop software, **MyNextbase Player**, and the **MyNextbase Connect** phone app do the job, but plenty of owners just want to put the card in a laptop and look.

## How Nextbase names its files

`240315_143022_001_FH.MP4`

- `240315` is the date, with a two-digit year (15 March 2024)
- `143022` is the time
- `001` is a sequence number
- `F` or `R` is the front or rear camera, and the last letter (`H` or `L`) is the quality

Protected clips (from a bump or the red button) go into a `Protected` folder. Rear camera modules add their own files with `R` in the name.

## Watching Nextbase footage with Tripframe

[Tripframe](/) recognises Nextbase names, including the two-digit year.

1. Put the microSD card in your computer.
2. Open [the app](/app/) in Chrome or Edge, or in Firefox or Safari through the folder picker.
3. Choose the card.

Every drive appears by day and time. Clips from the `Protected` folder are marked as events. If you have a rear camera module, its file is paired with the front file from the same moment, so both play together.

## Useful keys

- `Space` play and pause
- `,` and `.` step one frame back or forward
- `S` save the current frame as a PNG
- `R` record the view (both cameras in the current layout) as a new video
- `?` show all shortcuts

## GPS

Nextbase stores GPS in its own format, which Tripframe does not read yet. The video plays fully; the map panel stays empty. If you need the route, MyNextbase Player shows it.

## Nothing to install

Tripframe is a web page, so it works on a work laptop, a family computer or a Chromebook. The files are read locally and never uploaded.
