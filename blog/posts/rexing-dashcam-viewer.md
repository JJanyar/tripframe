Rexing is a US brand with a wide range of affordable dashcams, from the classic V1 to multi-channel models for rideshare drivers. Rexing offers its own GPS player and a phone app, but many owners just want a quick, clean overview of what is on the card.

## How Rexing files are named

Most Rexing cameras run on chipsets that name files after the date and time of recording, for example `2024_0315_143022_F.MP4`, with `F` and `R` for the front and rear camera. Locked event clips usually sit in a separate event or read-only folder.

## Watching Rexing footage with Tripframe

[Tripframe](/) reads the date and time from these names and puts every clip on a timeline per day.

1. Put the microSD card in your computer.
2. Open [the app](/app/) in Chrome or Edge.
3. Choose the card.

Front and rear files from the same moment play together. Event clips are labelled based on their folder. If your model uses a naming scheme without a date, Tripframe falls back to the time the file was written.

## GPS

Rexing models with a GPS mount often store GPS data in the same way as Viofo. When they do, Tripframe shows the route and speed on a map. If yours does not, the clip still plays without the map.

## Why use a browser?

- Nothing to install, so it works on any laptop
- Nothing is uploaded: the files are read on your computer
- Frame-by-frame stepping and PNG stills (`,` `.` and `S`)
