The mid-range is where most people should buy. For roughly $150 to $300 you get a front and rear camera, a sensor that holds up at night, GPS and a parking mode that works with a hardwire kit. Spending more mostly buys cloud features and brand polish.

Prices are rough street prices at the time of writing.

## Our picks

| Camera | Front | Rear | GPS | Rough price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Viofo A229 Pro 2CH | 4K, STARVIS 2 | 1440p, STARVIS 2 | in mount | $250–300 |
| Viofo A229 Plus 2CH | 1440p, STARVIS 2 | 1440p, STARVIS 2 | in mount | $180–220 |
| 70mai Dash Cam 4K A810 | 4K, STARVIS 2 | optional | built in | $150–220 |
| Nextbase 622GW | 4K | optional module | built in | $250–300 |

### Viofo A229 Pro: the all-rounder

A 4K front camera and a 1440p rear camera, both with STARVIS 2 sensors, plus HDR, GPS in the mount and Wi-Fi. It records clear plates by day and holds up well at night. It is the camera we tested Tripframe with most. Read [how to view Viofo footage](/blog/viofo-dashcam-viewer/).

### Viofo A229 Plus: best value

The same body as the Pro with 1440p on both ends. Night footage is excellent because the sensors gather a lot of light per pixel. If you drive mostly at night, it is arguably the better pick.

### 70mai A810: 4K for less

A strong 4K front camera at a lower price, with built-in GPS. The rear camera is an optional extra. 70mai's software is app-only, so for a computer you will want a separate viewer; see [how to view 70mai footage](/blog/70mai-dashcam-viewer/).

### Nextbase 622GW: easiest for beginners

A touchscreen, a magnetic mount that is easy to take off, image stabilisation and an emergency SOS feature (subscription). It is older than the others, but very easy to live with. [How to view Nextbase footage](/blog/nextbase-dashcam-viewer/).

## What matters most in this range

- **Rear camera quality.** A 1440p rear camera with a good sensor reads plates behind you; a 1080p one often does not at night.
- **Parking mode.** Needs a hardwire kit to the fuse box. Read [dashcam parking mode explained](/blog/dashcam-parking-mode-explained/).
- **Codec.** H.265 saves space but needs the right browser support: [how to play H.265 dashcam video](/blog/play-h265-hevc-dashcam-video/).
