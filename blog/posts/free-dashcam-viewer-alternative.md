**Dashcam Viewer**, by Earthshine Software, is one of the best-known programs for watching dashcam footage on Windows and Mac. It supports more than 140 camera models and is especially strong on GPS. It comes in three tiers: a free version that opens two files at a time, Plus and Pro. It is a desktop install.

If you want something free that you can use right now, without installing anything, [Tripframe](/) is an alternative. Here is how they compare, feature by feature.

## Where Tripframe is the better choice

- **You want to look at footage now.** Open a web page, choose the card, done.
- **You are on a computer you cannot install software on**, like a work laptop.
- **You want everything free**, without a limit on files, markers or loud-moment searches.
- **You care about open source.** The code is [on GitHub](https://github.com/JJanyar/tripframe).

## Where Dashcam Viewer is the better choice

- **Your brand's GPS format is not supported by Tripframe yet**, such as Thinkware, Nextbase or Garmin. Dashcam Viewer reads GPS from many more models.
- **You prefer a desktop program** that works without a browser.
