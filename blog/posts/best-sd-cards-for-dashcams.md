The memory card is the most common reason a dashcam fails to record the one moment that matters. A dashcam writes to it every second you drive, overwriting the oldest footage again and again. Ordinary cards made for phones and cameras are not built for that.

## What to buy: high-endurance cards

Endurance cards are rated for thousands of hours of continuous recording. These are all solid choices:

| Card | Why |
|---|---|
| Samsung PRO Endurance | Very long rated recording time, reliable, widely available |
| SanDisk High Endurance | Good value, easy to find everywhere |
| SanDisk Max Endurance | SanDisk's toughest card, for 4K and multi-channel |
| Kingston High Endurance | Solid alternative, often cheaper |
| Transcend High Endurance | Popular with dashcam makers, good warranty |
| Western Digital Purple (microSD) | Built for surveillance cameras, handles constant writing well |

Many dashcam makers also sell their own branded endurance cards; they are fine too.

### Avoid

- Cards sold as "Extreme" or "Ultra" for phones and action cams: fast, but not built for endless overwriting
- Unbranded or suspiciously cheap high-capacity cards: fake capacity is common
- Very old cards moved over from another device

## How much capacity do you need?

A simple rule: **gigabytes per hour ≈ bitrate in Mbps × 0.45**.

| Setup | Typical total bitrate | Per hour | 128 GB holds | 256 GB holds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1080p front only | 12–15 Mbps | ~6 GB | ~20 h | ~40 h |
| 1440p front + rear | 30–40 Mbps | ~16 GB | ~8 h | ~16 h |
| 4K front + 1440p rear | 50–70 Mbps | ~27 GB | ~4.5 h | ~9 h |
| 3 channels, 4K front | 70–90 Mbps | ~36 GB | ~3.5 h | ~7 h |

Check your camera's manual for the largest card it supports; many accept 256 or 512 GB.

## Keep the card healthy

- Format the card in the camera, not on your computer: see [how often to format a dashcam SD card](/blog/how-often-format-dashcam-sd-card/).
- Replace it every year or two with daily use, or when the camera reports card errors.
- Copy important clips off the card straight away; the camera will overwrite them.

## Checking what is on the card

Put the card in your computer and open it in [Tripframe](/app/). You see straight away whether the camera has been recording every day, which is the best health check there is.
