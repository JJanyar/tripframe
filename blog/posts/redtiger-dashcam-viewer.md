Redtiger sells some of the most popular budget 4K dashcams online, the F7N family in particular: a 4K front camera, a 1080p rear camera and Wi-Fi, at a low price. Redtiger offers a phone app for quick access, but a whole trip is much easier to review on a big screen.

## How Redtiger files are named

Redtiger cameras name files after the date and time of recording, followed by the camera, for example `2024_0315_143022_F.MP4` and `..._R.MP4`. Locked clips go into a separate event folder.

## Watching Redtiger footage with Tripframe

[Tripframe](/) reads these names.

1. Put the microSD card in your computer.
2. Open [the app](/app/) in Chrome or Edge.
3. Choose the card.

Every clip appears by day and time. The front and rear file of the same moment form one tile and play together: side by side, picture in picture, or one camera at a time. Event clips are labelled.

## A 4K front camera deserves frame stepping

With a 4K front camera, a plate is often readable, but only on one or two frames. Pause, step with `,` and `.`, and press `S` to save the sharpest frame as a PNG.

## GPS

If your Redtiger has a GPS module and stores its data the Novatek way, Tripframe draws the route and speed on a map. Otherwise the clip plays without the map.

## H.265

Some Redtiger models can record in H.265. If a clip stays black, read [how to play H.265 dashcam video](/blog/play-h265-hevc-dashcam-video/).
