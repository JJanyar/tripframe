Above about $300, dashcams stop competing on basic image quality, which is already good in the [mid-range](/blog/best-mid-range-dashcams/). What you pay for is everything around it: cloud access while you are away from the car, smarter parking mode, higher frame rates and a cleaner install.

Prices are rough street prices at the time of writing, without subscriptions.

## Our picks

| Camera | Strong point | Front | Cloud | Rough price |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BlackVue DR970X-2CH Plus | Cloud and design | 4K | yes (Wi-Fi or LTE add-on) | $450–550 |
| Thinkware U3000 | Radar parking mode | 4K | yes (with LTE) | $400–500 |
| Viofo A329 | 4K at 60 fps | 4K | no | $350–400 |
| Nextbase iQ | Built-in LTE and app | 4K | yes (subscription) | $400–500 |
| Garmin Dash Cam X310 | 4K with polariser | 4K | via Garmin app | $300–350 |

### BlackVue DR970X Plus: best for cloud features

Clean cylindrical design, excellent parking mode and the most mature cloud service: see live view and get notified of impacts from your phone. [How to view BlackVue footage](/blog/blackvue-dashcam-viewer/).

### Thinkware U3000: best parking mode

Uses radar to detect movement around the car, so it can stay in a low-power state until something actually approaches. That means far longer parking coverage on the same battery. [How to view Thinkware footage](/blog/thinkware-dashcam-viewer/).

### Viofo A329: best image

4K at 60 frames per second gives you twice as many frames to pause on, which makes reading a moving plate much easier. No cloud, but the best raw footage in this list for the money. [How to view Viofo footage](/blog/viofo-dashcam-viewer/).

### Nextbase iQ: the connected car camera

Built-in LTE, a polished app, remote viewing and features such as guardian mode. Most of the smart features need a subscription. [How to view Nextbase footage](/blog/nextbase-dashcam-viewer/).

### Garmin Dash Cam X310: compact and premium

A 4K camera with a built-in polarising filter to cut windscreen glare, a touchscreen and Garmin's reliable app. [How to view Garmin footage](/blog/garmin-dashcam-viewer/).

## Who should buy high-end?

- You park in public places and want alerts on your phone
- You need long parking coverage without draining the car battery
- You drive for work and want the best evidence quality

If none of that applies, a mid-range Viofo or 70mai gives you 90% of the footage quality for half the price.
